Never Late

Never Late is a Chrome extension that helps users avoid missing online meetings by monitoring their Google Calendar and alerting them before meetings start.

How Never Late Works

  • User signs in with their Google account
  • The extension reads upcoming Google Calendar events
  • It detects meetings that contain Google Meet links
  • Users receive reminders before meetings begin
  • The extension can automatically open the meeting at start time

Why Google Calendar Access Is Required

Never Late requires access to Google Calendar in order to:

  • Read upcoming calendar events
  • Identify meeting start times
  • Extract Google Meet links from events
  • Trigger notifications and automatic meeting opening

Privacy & Data Usage

Never Late only accesses Google Calendar data after explicit user consent. Calendar data is used solely to provide meeting reminders and automation. No data is shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Users can revoke access at any time from their Google Account settings.
